# The Space Between Stars: Physics-Informed ML

When training deep learning models on physical phenomena — fluid dynamics, structural stress, aerodynamic drag — data is notoriously scarce and expensive to acquire. Sensor placement is limited, wind tunnel hours are costly, and simulations take days.

Purely data-driven models struggle when forced to extrapolate beyond their training distribution. They hallucinate non-physical behavior, breaking conservation of mass or energy.

## Enter Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs)

Physics-Informed Neural Networks solve sparse data challenges by constraining the network's hypothesis space using known governing Partial Differential Equations (PDEs).

Instead of treating the loss function purely as a mean-squared error between predictions and ground truth labels:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{data}} + \lambda \mathcal{L}_{\text{physics}}$$

The physics loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{physics}}$ measures how severely the neural network violates the governing PDE evaluated at residual collocation points using automatic differentiation (Autograd).

```python
import torch

def physics_loss(model, x_collocation):
    x_collocation.requires_grad_(True)
    u = model(x_collocation)
    
    # Compute derivative du/dx via autograd
    u_x = torch.autograd.grad(u, x_collocation, torch.ones_like(u), create_graph=True)[0]
    u_xx = torch.autograd.grad(u_x, x_collocation, torch.ones_like(u_x), create_graph=True)[0]
    
    # Governing 1D heat equation residual: u_t - alpha * u_xx = 0
    residual = u_x - 0.01 * u_xx
    return torch.mean(residual ** 2)
```

## Real-world eVTOL Structural Optimization

During my research at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc) under Dr. Harursampath, we applied PINNs to structural blade optimization for electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft.

By embedding beam theory boundary constraints directly into PyTorch loss objectives, we achieved accurate stress prediction with **90% fewer experimental data points** than traditional surrogate models.
