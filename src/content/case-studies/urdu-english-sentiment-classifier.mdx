## Executive Summary

Customer reviews in South Asia heavily feature code-switched Roman Urdu (Urdu written in Latin script) mixed with English phrases, rendering standard Western NLP sentiment tools ineffective.

We constructed a fine-tuned bilingual BERT variant trained on a custom 12,000-sample annotated dataset of code-switched Roman Urdu and English customer feedback, achieving 89% classification accuracy and winning the **#1 Best Commercial Capstone Award** at GIFT University.

---

## Model Pipeline & Dataset Curation

1. **Custom Tokenization**: Customized WordPiece tokenization vocabulary to preserve Roman Urdu phonetics and colloquial expressions.
2. **Transfer Learning**: Fine-tuned XLM-RoBERTa base weights with PyTorch and AdamW optimizer using warm-up cosine decay schedules.

## Impact & Recognition

- **89% Classification Accuracy**: Outperformed general multilingual models by 24 percentage points on Roman Urdu sentiment tasks.
