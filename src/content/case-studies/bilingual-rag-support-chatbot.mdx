## Executive Summary

A major retail client faced overwhelming customer support volume across mixed English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu languages. Support ticket resolution averaged 42 minutes, creating high operational backlog and customer churn.

At **Confiz**, we engineered a bilingual **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)** support chatbot powered by INT8 quantized embeddings, FAISS vector index, and async FastAPI services that handled 40,000+ monthly conversations and reduced ticket resolution time from 42 to 9 minutes (78% reduction).

---

## Technical Highlights

1. **Bilingual Embedding Model**: Fine-tuned multilingual sentence transformers on 12,000+ domain support conversations in English, Urdu, and Roman Urdu script.
2. **Async Microservices**: Built async **FastAPI** microservice with **Celery** task queues and Redis caching, scaling throughput 8× (12 → 95 req/s).
3. **Confidence Escalation Routing**: Automatically routed ambiguous queries (&lt; 75% similarity confidence) to live human agents with full conversation context.

---

## Business Impact

- **78% Resolution Time Cut**: Decreased average support resolution from 42 to 9 minutes.
- **40,000+ Monthly Inquiries**: Automated 65% of routine tier-1 support tickets without human intervention.
- **40% Vector Cost Savings**: Quantized embedding weights from FP32 to INT8, reducing vector store memory footprint.
