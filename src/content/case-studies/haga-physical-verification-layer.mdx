## Executive Summary

Generative world models (e.g. Sora, CogVideoX) and neural robot policies frequently hallucinate physical impossibilities during policy rollout—such as objects hovering mid-air, instant momentum inversion, or interpenetrating rigid bodies.

At **Haga Labs**, we architected an automated verification and benchmarking harness (`haga-core`) that stress-tests policy trajectories in MuJoCo and Robosuite against physical constraints, paired with an automated video physics violation detector.

---

## The Challenge

1. **Unphysical World Model Rollouts**: Generative video models lack explicit 3D momentum and friction constraints.
2. **Policy Brittleness**: Embodied AI policy rollouts fail under unseen friction degradation or payload weight variations.
3. **Lack of Standardized Auditability**: Roboticists and investors lack a verified audit trail before deploying policies to physical hardware.

---

## System Architecture & Approach

### 1. Trajectory Stress Engine (`haga-core`)
- Built in **JAX** and **PyTorch**, executing mass, friction, and joint limit degradation curves in **MuJoCo**.
- Evaluates policy robustness across 100+ perturbed simulation environments.

### 2. Video Physics Violation Detector
- Analyzes video policy rollouts (e.g. CogVideoX outputs) by tracking optical flow, object velocity vectors, and gravity vector consistency.
- Automatically flags teleportation, ungrounded hovering, and mass-balance anomalies.

### 3. Public Evidence Browser (`haga-web`)
- Full-stack evidence portal built with **Bun** and **Next.js**, displaying interactive physics violation overlays and investor data rooms.

---

## Key Results & Impact

- **100% Precision / Recall**: Validated on synthetic physics violation test suites.
- **Sub-15ms Validation**: Frame-by-frame physics metric calculations enabling real-time telemetry filtering.
- **Data Room Evidence**: Deployed interactive evidence browser for automated policy auditability.
